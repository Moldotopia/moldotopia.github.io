Delete Your Data

Effective Date: June 13, 2026

How to delete your data

INTIMO is local-first: most of your content lives only on your device, and anything you share with a partner is end-to-end encrypted. Deleting your data therefore has two parts.

1. Delete your on-device data

In the app, open Settings and tap Reset All Data, then confirm. This permanently deletes all of your local data — activities, reflections, wishes, preferences, encryption keys, and pairing information — from your device. This cannot be undone.

2. Delete your server-side data

INTIMO stores very little on its servers: your device registration and push-notification preferences. To remove it:

  • Find your User ID: in the app, open Settings → About Intimo; your User ID is shown there.
  • Request deletion: email your User ID to intimo.support@gmail.com with the subject “Delete my data”. We process requests within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • On your device (via Reset All Data): activities, reflections, wishes, planner data, cycle entries, preferences, encryption keys, and pairing information.
  • On our servers (via your request): device registration and push-notification preferences.

What may be retained

  • Aggregated, anonymized analytics that cannot identify you.
  • Minimal records we are required to keep by law.

Important

Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. Content you previously shared with a partner is also stored on your partner’s device under their own account, and is governed by their choices.

Contact

Questions about data deletion: intimo.support@gmail.com
